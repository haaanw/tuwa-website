For self-coached athletes

Your plan. Made safe and optimal.

Tuwa is the sports-science back room for athletes who coach themselves. It reads your body — HRV, sleep, resting heart rate, training history — and modulates the plan you wrote: today’s numbers, a go / modify / hold verdict, and where your load is trending. It never writes your program.

Built for athletes who train sport skill and strength in parallel.

01 · Verdict

Today’s decision, made concrete.

Every morning Tuwa turns your readiness into one verdict — with the exact adjustments to make. Load drops in real numbers, microdose options when a full session is too much, and a strike-zone bar showing where today should land.

02 · Load

One fatigue budget.

Court time, barbell work and conditioning all draw from the same body. Tuwa tracks them as one load — acute against chronic — keeps you inside the strike zone, and flags overreach while it is still a trend, not a setback.

Load trend acute : chronic

03 · Recovery

A score with its reasons.

HRV, sleep and resting heart rate are read against your own baselines, then folded into one readiness score with plain-language reasons. No black box — you see which signal moved and what it means for today.

Baselines adapt as you do. A hard week shifts the goalposts; the score knows the difference between tired and detrained.

Privacy

Your health data stays on your phone.

Tuwa reads HRV, sleep and resting heart rate from Apple Health on-device. Raw health data is never uploaded — only composite scores sync between your devices.
