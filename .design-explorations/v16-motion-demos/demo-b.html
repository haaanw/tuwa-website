Training load

One fatigue budget for everything you train.

Court sessions, lifting and conditioning all draw from the same recovery reserves. Tuwa tracks them as one load, watches your acute-to-chronic ratio, and forecasts overreach before it arrives.

Acute : chronic workload ratio

Trending hot — time to modify

The ratio compares the last seven days of load to the last four weeks. Tuwa's job is to keep it inside the strike zone.
